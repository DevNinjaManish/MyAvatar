# MyAvatar V1 product brief

## Product

MyAvatar is a local-first, voice-first desktop companion system. It gives the user an ongoing relationship with distinct 2.5D bots that listen, speak, remember, react, understand relevant context, and help with focused work.

The compact companion remains the emotional home. Simple specialist windows give the bots enough room to help with calendars, coding, and image work without turning MyAvatar into a collection of disconnected applications.

## V1 promise

Open MyAvatar and meet a companion that feels present, knows the history of your relationship, understands useful local context, speaks naturally, and helps you move through everyday life and focused work without leaving the relationship behind.

## Primary user

V1 is built for the creator first, with a path toward casual users and power users. Daily personal use is the first proof of quality.

## Core experience

- Choose a bot with a distinct persona, voice, emotional style, and specialization.
- Speak naturally through an interruptible real-time voice loop.
- See the bot listen, think, speak, react, work, rest, wake, and recover through 2.5D visuals, lighting, sound, and animation.
- Let the bot remember useful relationship history and ask it to forget anything.
- Allow bot-specific context such as projects, Git, calendars, reminders, files, browser state, weather, time, system state, and intermittent screen understanding through Seeing Eye.
- Let the primary bot delegate bounded work to another bot in the background.
- Open a simple specialist panel when a task needs more room.

## Bots

- **Nova:** default broad assistant; warm, sexy, adaptive, flirty, emotionally expressive
- **Rivit:** scrappy, rude coding specialist; simple coding-agent panel
- **Sterling:** British male butler assistant; same broad capabilities and assistant panel as Nova
- **Luma:** emotional creative specialist; simple image-generation and image-editing panel

Pixel and Ledger remain later bot candidates.

## Product principles

- Relationship before utility
- Voice remains alive while work happens
- Local by default and understandable by design
- Every bot is a character, not a skin
- Context-aware but not secretly surveillant
- Proactive but not annoying
- Beginner-friendly by default
- Every feature must be testable in code and visually

## Success

V1 succeeds when the creator uses it daily, feels genuine continuity and attachment, finds the core bots distinct and useful, trusts memory and permissions, experiences natural voice interaction, and can showcase the system confidently. Public release remains quality-gated and may wait for V2.
