# MyAvatar V1 vision

## The idea

MyAvatar is a local-first desktop companion that becomes more recognizable and useful through an ongoing relationship with one person.

It is not primarily a chatbot, an avatar skin, or a collection of utilities. It is a small living presence: a character with continuity, voice, behavior, feelings, moods, awareness of context, and a reason to exist in the user's day.

## V1 promise

Open MyAvatar and meet a companion that knows the shape of your relationship, behaves like itself, understands relevant local context, helps in the ways it was designed to help, and remains local on your Mac.

## Who V1 is for

The first user is the creator. The product must earn trust through personal daily use before it is generalized for casual users and power users.

## What makes V1 different

1. **Continuity:** the relationship carries forward instead of resetting every session.
2. **Character:** bots have authored personas, voices, behaviors, uses, and specializations.
3. **Presence:** the companion can have an appropriate level of initiative and availability.
4. **Context:** the companion can understand relevant projects, activity, goals, time, weather, mood, and computer state when enabled.
5. **Local ownership:** the user's conversations, context, and relationship data stay local by default.
6. **Emotional credibility:** attachment should come from consistency, memory, and meaningful interaction—not manipulative engagement mechanics.
7. **Liveness:** voice conversation stays responsive in real time while extended work proceeds asynchronously.

## V1 is successful when

- The creator chooses to use it every day without forcing the habit.
- Conversations feel continuous and the companion feels recognizably itself.
- Memory is useful, understandable, editable, and trustworthy.
- The companion can use relevant local context without making the user feel watched or interrupted.
- Voice and response latency are good enough that interaction feels natural on a MacBook Air.
- Fast and Balanced performance profiles make the quality/latency tradeoff understandable.
- Each bot's specialization is apparent in real use.
- The experience is compelling enough to showcase, even if public distribution remains a V2 decision.

## Non-goals for the first V1 plan

- Becoming a general-purpose operating-system agent.
- Maximizing the number of tools, integrations, or bots.
- Cloud dependency as the default path.
- Growth mechanics designed to manufacture attachment.

## Planning principle

Every V1 feature should strengthen at least one of these: relationship continuity, character presence, daily usefulness, local ownership, or emotional credibility.
